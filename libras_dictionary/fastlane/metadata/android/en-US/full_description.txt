Brazilian Sign Language (Libras) dictionary that brings several public sources together in one place.

Type a word in Portuguese and the app searches for the corresponding sign in:

• INES — National Institute of Deaf Education (Brazil)
• Rede Surdos — Federal University of Ceará (UFC)
• UFV Libras Dictionary — Federal University of Viçosa
• Libras Acadêmica — Fluminense Federal University (UFF)
• SpreadTheSign — European Sign Language Centre

Results show the sign video, description, usage examples and the Libras gloss when available. You can also open a YouTube search for signs not found in the dictionaries.

Features:
• Simultaneous search across sources, with per-source filters
• In-app sign video playback
• Light and dark theme
• Sign sharing
• No ads, no tracking, no personal data collection

The app is free software (CC0). Video and content rights belong to the original institutions; usage is for educational and accessibility purposes.
